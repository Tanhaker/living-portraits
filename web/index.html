What happens to the photo

Mend

GFPGAN repairs blur and damage on the face. It is blended with the original at 60%, so they still look like themselves.

Colour

DDColor predicts colour for black-and-white and sepia prints. The light and detail stay from the original photo.

Depth

Depth Anything V2 estimates what is near and far. The page uses it for candlelight and gentle movement.

Check

An ArcFace face-recognition model compares the face before and after. The score is shown under every portrait.

What it cannot do

  • Know the real colours. Every colour is the model's guess.
  • Restore side profiles as well as faces looking at the camera.
  • Move, re-pose or give words to a face. It is built not to.
