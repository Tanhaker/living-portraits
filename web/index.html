What happens to the photo

Mend

GFPGAN repairs blur and damage on the face, blended gently so they still look like themselves.

Colour

DDColor suggests colour for black-and-white prints. The light and detail stay from the original.

Depth

Depth Anything finds what is near and far, so candlelight and your cursor give the photo depth.

Check

A face-recognition model compares before and after, to make sure it is still the same person.

The face is never moved, re-posed or given words. Everything runs on open-source models on our own server, so family photos are never sent to an AI company.

Built for Día de los Muertos with GFPGAN, DDColor, Depth Anything V2 and facexlib, running on DigitalOcean.
